Stagecoach 5×5 Transit Risk Matrix Heatmap

Formula: Risk (R) = Consequence/Severity (S) × Likelihood (L) conforming to PeopleSafe / UK HSE guidelines.

17–25: Extreme 10–16: High 5–9: Medium 1–4: Low
Severity $\downarrow$ \ Likelihood $\rightarrow$ 1: Rare 2: Unlikely 3: Possible 4: Likely 5: Almost Certain
Ri: Initial Pre-Control Hazard Count Rr: Residual Post-Control Hazard Count

Operational Action Thresholds

EXTREME (17–25): ROUTE / VEHICLE PROHIBITED

Immediate route or vehicle restriction. Double-deck bans on low bridges <4.5m or physical highway bans until statutory remediation.

HIGH (10–16): MANDATORY VEHICLE & SPEED CONTROLS

Vehicle size locks (Solo / SWB Midi only). Speed reduction notices (20/30mph). Ticket Machine (ETM) cab warning beacons.

MEDIUM (5–9): CAUTION & ROUTE LEARNING ZONE

Mandatory driver route card briefing, primary lane road position, and speed ceilings.

LOW (1–4): STANDARD TRANSIT OPERATION

Standard Highway Code and Stagecoach Defensive Driving Protocol.
