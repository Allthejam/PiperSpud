Operational Action Thresholds

EXTREME (17–25): ROUTE PROHIBITED

Immediate road ban. No vehicle permitted until statutory engineering controls or diversions in place.

HIGH (10–16): RESTRICTED ACCESS

Mandatory vehicle size locks (e.g. Solo/SWB only). Council tree cutting/signage escalation. ETM driver warning alerts.

MEDIUM (5–9): CAUTION ZONE

Mandated route learning, defensive driving line, reduced speeds (15–20 mph), primary lane road position.

LOW (1–4): STANDARD OPERATION

General Highway Code compliance and standard Stagecoach Defensive Driving Rules.

SERVICE 133: GRANTOWN CIRCULAR

AUTHORISED VEHICLES: OPTARE SOLO / SHORT WHEELBASE EVORA ONLY

DOUBLE DECKERS STRICTLY BANNED

1. Castle Grant Railway Arch (16'0" / 4.87m)

Masonry arch bridge. Clearance tapers sharply towards near-side kerbs. Approach at maximum 15 mph. Position vehicle into centre road arch if traffic allows. Anticipate high HGVs taking road centre.

2. Cottartown to Tomvaich Single-Track

NEVER drop wheels onto soft grass verges. Deep ditches alongside. Stop in primary paved position; force oncoming cars into passing places. Watch for wandering farm livestock.

3. Braes of Castle Grant Hairpin

Severe gradient & acute bend. First gear crawling speed (< 8 mph). Position away from offside edge. Watch for winter low-sun blinding on crest and rear tail-swing on barriers.

4. Advie Spey Bridge & A95 Fast Trunk

Bridge has deep gullies on both parapets and blind approaches. At A95 junction, come to a complete halt; ensure 300m clearance in both directions before pulling onto 60 mph trunk road.
